🧮 1. OPENAI: AI CLAIMS PROOFS IN MATH, COMPUTER SCIENCE & PHYSICS (CLAIM, NOT YET REVIEWED)
On Oct 26, OpenAI said its models proved the local smoothing conjecture in every dimension – which would also resolve the famous Kakeya and Stein restriction conjectures. It also announced a claimed refutation of the Exponential Time Hypothesis (computer science) and a Yang–Mills result (physics, manuscript not yet released). The math proofs come with computer-checked Lean formalizations, but none of the results have been independently peer-reviewed yet.

Google DeepMind's Genie 4 (research preview, Oct 27) turns a text prompt into an interactive world – now with native sound, music and dialogue, and you can steer it live, e.g. change the weather while you play. Access is still limited.
