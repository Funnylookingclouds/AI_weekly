## Ep. 2 – "Bag C Is Empty" · SKRAMMEL (chest of drawers) · ~85 words
1. **HOOK** `[Pia sorts screws into labelled cups – happy, sparkle]`
   PIA: Bag A: twenty-four. Bag B: sixteen. Bag C…
   `[shakes an empty bag – surprised, shock]` PIA: …Bag C is EMPTY.
2. DUKE `[smug, sparkle]`: Relax. Who needs bag C?
   `{manual pages flip – Mr. Manual points at a big "C" on every page; STEP counter}`
   PIA `[angry, storm]`: EVERY step needs bag C!
3. `{search montage: drawers fly, packing peanuts storm when Duke shakes the box, clock spins wildly, scoreboard "SCREWS FOUND: 0"}`
   DUKE `[angry, rage]`: YOU lost it!  `{VS card DUKE vs PIA}`
   PIA `[angry, grawlix]`: I LABEL things, Duke!
4. ALVIN `[from the pile – smug]`: Has anyone checked… the big guy?
5. `{camera whip-pans to Gus, chewing happily – love}` GUS: Mmm. Crunchy.
   `{record scratch – everyone freezes, shock}`
6. `{BURP! burst – a screw flies out and KA-CHUNKs into the dresser}`
   GUS `[happy, joy]`: Oh! Was that bag C?
   PIA `[angry, grawlix]`: Hashtag-at-percent… `{bleep}`
7. `{BURP–KA-CHUNK ×3 montage, dresser builds itself, STEP counter races to 12 / 12 ✔}`
   DUKE `[laugh, music]`: Best. Tool. Ever.
8. LEFTY `[rolls in]`: Uh… where do I go?
   `{Gus turns to Lefty – love, licks lips}` LEFTY `[scared, sweat]`: …Nope. `{Lefty rolls away fast}` → outro
