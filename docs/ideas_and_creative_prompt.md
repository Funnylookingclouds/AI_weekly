# AI Bounce – topic ideas & creative-director prompt

Researched 2026-11-22. ✅ = fact checked against a primary/official source in search results · ⚠️ = secondary sources only, verify before scripting.

## A. Special-edition topic ideas (ranked by reach potential)

### 1. "The move that shocked the world" – AlphaGo, Move 37 (2016)
* **Hook:** "One move had a 1-in-10,000 chance. An AI played it anyway."
* **Facts:** AlphaGo beat Lee Sedol 4–1 in Seoul, March 2016, watched by 200M+ people ✅. Lee had won 18 world titles ✅. Move 37 in game 2 had ~1/10,000 chance of being played by a human ✅; Lee answered with Move 78 in game 4 ("God's Touch"), equally rare, and won that game ✅. Go has ~10^170 board positions ✅.
* **Format:** Relay – Ture narrates the match like a sports commentator, Saga explains Move 37, Cara does Lee's comeback.
* **Visual:** Hand-drawn Go board where stones slap down on twos; "1 / 10,000" odds stamp; 200M audience counter.
* **Sources:** https://deepmind.google/research/breakthroughs/alphago/

### 2. "AI won a Nobel Prize" – AlphaFold (2024)
* **Hook:** "An AI helped win a Nobel Prize. Here's what it actually solved."
* **Facts:** 2024 Chemistry Nobel: half to David Baker, half shared by Demis Hassabis & John Jumper for protein structure prediction ✅. AlphaFold predicts a protein's 3D shape from its amino-acid sequence – a ~50-year-old problem ✅. Predictions are free; 2M+ researchers in 190 countries have used them ✅. "Virtually all ~200 million known proteins" ⚠️ (Academy wording via reposts – check nobelprize.org).
* **Format:** Single presenter (Cara – "grandma explains proteins").
* **Visual:** A paper string of beads folding into a 3D shape; Nobel medal stamp; world map with 190 pins.
* **Sources:** https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/chemistry/2024/press-release/ · https://blog.google/technology/google-deepmind/google-deepmind-demis-hassabis-john-jumper-nobel-prize-chemistry-alphafold/

### 3. "How much electricity does AI really use?" (myth vs. fact)
* **Hook:** "Is AI eating the world's electricity? The real number might surprise you."
* **Facts:** Data centres used ~485 TWh in 2025 (IEA, Apr 2026) ✅; AI-focused data centres ~155 TWh = ~0.5% of global electricity (Our World in Data, IEA data) ✅; IEA projects data centres ~950 TWh ≈ 3% of global demand by 2030 ✅. Independent Nature paper: AI could reach ~1% of global electricity by 2030 ✅.
* **Format:** Rune (sporty, data-nerd) vs. Ture (sceptical) – a friendly debate relay.
* **Visual:** Hand-drawn pie chart with a tiny AI slice; light-bulb icons; 2025 → 2030 bar growth.
* **Angle:** Balanced – small share today, fast growth. Great comment bait.
* **Sources:** https://ourworldindata.org/how-much-energy-do-data-centers-and-artificial-intelligence-use · https://www.carbonbrief.org/ai-five-charts-that-put-data-centre-energy-use-and-emissions-into-context · https://www.nature.com/articles/s44458-026-00152-5

### 4. "1.2 billion people a week" – how fast the world adopted AI
* **Hook:** "One in seven people on Earth now use one AI app every week."
* **Facts:** ChatGPT: 1.2B weekly active users (OpenAI DevDay, Sep 29 2026) ⚠️; 1B on Aug 6 2026 ⚠️; 900M in Feb 2026 ⚠️. (World population ≈ 8.2B → ~1 in 7.) Verify on OpenAI's own channels first.
* **Format:** Saga presents; growth counter.
* **Visual:** People icons filling a globe; a "1 in 7" crowd row with one highlighted.

### 5. "Can YOU spot the AI?" – interactive Turing-test quiz
* **Hook:** "One of these two answers was written by AI. Comment A or B."
* **Facts:** Builds on Turing's 1950 imitation game ✅ (our special #1). Script writes two short answers (one human-written by the owner, one AI) – reveal at the end or in the pinned comment.
* **Format:** Cara as quiz host; huge engagement potential (comments = reach).
* **Visual:** Two paper cards A/B, countdown clock, "REVEAL" stamp.

### 6. "Chess, 1997: the first time a machine beat the champion" – Deep Blue vs. Kasparov ⚠️
* Needs its own research pass (not verified in this round). Pairs naturally with #1 as a two-part "Machines vs. Champions" series.

### 7. Recurring segment: "Claim check"
* Follow up on earlier claims: "Remember the AI Riemann proof? Here's what experts found." Builds trust and a reason to subscribe. Run whenever a claim from wk 44/47 gets reviewed.

### 8. Recurring segment: "AI word of the week"
* 15-second explainer of one term (e.g. "time horizon", "world model", "open weights") by one presenter, one board. Cheap to make, good for search ("what is a world model").

## B. Creative-director prompt (copy into any AI chat)

Use this to get fresh visual ideas for boards, thumbnails, motion and new character poses. Paste the whole block, then add the episode script underneath.

```
You are the creative director and visual artist for "AI Bounce" (youtube.com/@AIBounce), a faceless YouTube Shorts channel: "What did AI solve this week?" – fast, jargon-free AI news in under 60 seconds.

BRAND & STYLE
- Look: hand-made paper-cutout collage. Torn cream paper boards (#f3ead6) with grain, ink line art (#2b2622), accents in teal (#1d6b73), mustard (#c98a1c), brick red (#b4483b), moss green (#4f7d3a). Handwritten labels (Caveat-style), bold sans headlines. Stop-motion movement "on twos" (~12 fps), boards slapped on with a little overshoot, rubber stamps that thump in.
- Intro/outro only: neon "AI BOUNCE" logo (cyan→purple→pink gradient, glowing bouncing ball with speed trails, tagline "KEEP UP WITH AI").
- Format: vertical 9:16 (1080×1920). Board 960×760 in the top half, a caption strip ("NAME: sentence") under it, the presenter in the bottom half. Keep the right 170 px and bottom 300 px free of key info (YouTube UI).

PRESENTERS (illustrated paper-sticker characters, rotate weekly)
- Rune – young man, dark hair, black rectangular glasses, navy/teal sports shirt with heart-rate strap, running tights; nerdy, precise.
- Ture – big older man, grey-brown hair, full ginger-grey beard, mustard puffer vest over dark knit sweater, cargo pants, wooden pendant; warm, jokey storyteller.
- Saga – young woman, blonde double braids, freckles, navy wool coat over cream knit, earbuds, boots; curious, quick.
- Cara – older woman, short grey curls, rosy cheeks, green cardigan, mustard scarf; kind, witty, "grandma explains".
Each has moods: body (full figure), happy, curious, serious. Scandinavian nature backdrops (lakes, birches, granite, red cottages), blurred.

RULES
- Accuracy first: never invent numbers; label unverified claims "CLAIM / UNDER REVIEW"; mark mock-ups "ILLUSTRATION"; no real company logos or real people's likenesses – use generic icons and name labels.
- Every 2–3 seconds something new must happen on screen.

YOUR TASK
For the script below, give me:
1. THREE different visual concepts (name + one-line idea each), from safe to wild.
2. For the best concept: a board-by-board storyboard – for each spoken beat: board title, handwritten sub-label, the drawing, what moves (stop-motion), which presenter mood, and one sound effect.
3. A thumbnail idea (one frame, ≤4 words of text, presenter expression).
4. One surprising "signature moment" (a visual gag or reveal viewers would screenshot).
5. If a new character pose would help (e.g. pointing, waving, holding a sign), write an image-generation prompt for it in this exact format:
   "Full-body illustration of [character description from above] [pose], hand-drawn ink line art with soft watercolour shading, paper-sticker style with a thick torn cream paper border, standing on a plain flat light-grey background (#d4d7dc), no shadow on background, no text, centred, 16:9."
   (Plain grey background is required so the pose can be cut out automatically.)

SCRIPT:
<paste script here>
```

### Why the pose prompt matters
New poses generated on a **plain light-grey background with the torn paper border** drop straight into `scripts/cutout_characters.py` (the flood-fill keyer) – clean cutouts, no manual masking. Most wanted next: **pointing at the board, waving hello/bye, holding a sign, thumbs-up, shocked face.**

* Prototype: `output/proto/AIBounce_joke_of_the_week_prototype.mp4` (9.3 s).

### Segment ideas
1. **AI joke of the week (Bouncy)** – setup on the board, punchline slams in, "BA-DUM-TSS!" + rimshot + confetti, Bouncy laughs. ~7–9 s at the end, before the outro.
2. **Bouncy's reaction cam** – Bouncy pops into a corner on the biggest number of the week with a "WOW" face + comic burst. 1 s, no extra script.
3. **True or False?** – viewers guess before the reveal; giant TRUE/FALSE rubber stamp. Great for comments.
4. **AI fail of the week** – a real, sourced funny AI mistake (needs verification every week).
5. **Then vs. Now** – split board "2016 vs 2026" with a paper wipe between the halves.
6. **Guess the number** – countdown dial, "comment your guess", reveal next episode (retention + return viewers).
