AI just beat eight brain tumor experts at their own job – plus Google DeepMind's AI mathematically checked Signal's encryption code. The latest AI breakthroughs of the week in under a minute. 🧠🔒🧬

🧠 1. AI BEATS 8 SPECIALISTS AT DIAGNOSING BRAIN TUMORS
At the AMP 2026 pathology conference, Mayo Clinic's Kenneth Aldape presented a test where an AI classified 100 brain tumor cases from tissue images (choosing from 52 tumor types). The AI reached 97% accuracy and outperformed all eight neuropathologists – and specialists became more accurate when the AI assisted them. Note: conference presentation, small study, not yet peer-reviewed.
🔗 https://www.insideprecisionmedicine.com/topics/molecular-dx/amp-2026-is-human-expertise-limiting-ai-diagnostics/

🔒 2. ALPHAPROOF CODE – AI VERIFIES SIGNAL'S ENCRYPTION
Google DeepMind's AlphaProof Code formally verified key security properties of libsignal, the encryption library behind Signal's one-to-one chats, down to machine code – millions of lines of machine-checked proof (Lean) in four weeks. When researchers reintroduced two previously fixed bugs, it caught both. (Covers the core library, not the whole app; results reported by DeepMind.)
🔗 https://deepmind.google/blog/introducing-alphaproof-code/
🔗 https://thenextweb.com/news/google-deepmind-alphaproof-code-signal-encryption-formal-verification

🧬 3. ALPHAGENOME 2 – READING DNA CELL BY CELL
DeepMind's AlphaGenome 2 predicts how DNA variants change gene activity in specific cell types, finding nearly twice as many gene-changing variants as the previous best model – clues to where genetic diseases begin. (Research use only.)
🔗 https://deepmind.google/blog/alphagenome-2-deciphering-how-cells-read-dna-to-better-understand-disease/
🔗 https://www.techrepublic.com/article/news-google-deepmind-alphagenome-2-genetic-variants/
