- Lee Sedol: winner of 18 world titles.
- Move 37, game 2: ~1 in 10,000 chance of being played; helped AlphaGo win; "upended centuries of traditional wisdom".
- Move 78, game 4 ("God's Touch"): also ~1 in 10,000; helped Lee win that game.
- Go ≈ 10^170 board configurations ("a googol times more complex than chess") vs ~10^80 atoms in the observable universe.
- Commentators' initial "mistake" reaction: widely reported live reaction (wording "first think" kept soft).
