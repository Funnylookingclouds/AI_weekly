## Facts & sources
1. Brain tumor AI – presented at AMP 2026 (~Nov 13) by Kenneth Aldape (Mayo Clinic). 100 cases, 52 classes, H&E images + basic patient info. AI 97 % vs all 8 neuropathologists. AI also improved specialists' accuracy as an aid. Conference presentation, not peer-reviewed; small test.
   - https://www.insideprecisionmedicine.com/topics/molecular-dx/amp-2026-is-human-expertise-limiting-ai-diagnostics/
2. AlphaProof Code – announced Tue Nov 10 (DeepMind blog). Formally verified selected security properties of libsignal (one-to-one chats), down to machine code, proofs checked by Lean. Sources disagree on size (3M vs 6.5M lines) → script says "millions". Caught 2 reintroduced historical bugs. Not the full app; DeepMind's own claims.
   - https://deepmind.google/blog/introducing-alphaproof-code/

3. AlphaGenome 2 – announced Wed Nov 11. Predicts how DNA variants affect gene activity per cell type; finds nearly twice as many expression-changing variants as previous best model. Research use only.
   - https://deepmind.google/blog/alphagenome-2-deciphering-how-cells-read-dna-to-better-understand-disease/
