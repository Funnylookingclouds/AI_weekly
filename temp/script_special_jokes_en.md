| Hook | Can AI actually be funny? Let's compare its jokes – then and now. |
| 2023 | Back in **2023**, researchers asked ChatGPT for a joke **one thousand and eight** times. Over **ninety percent** were the same **twenty-five jokes**. The number one hit: *Why did the scarecrow win an award? Because he was outstanding in his field.* |
| 2023 #2 | Number two: *Why did the tomato turn red? It saw the salad dressing.* |
| 2024 | One year later, a new study found **most people** rated ChatGPT's jokes **funnier than human ones**. |
| 2026 | So here's a **2026** joke, **written by AI**: *I told my AI to stop overthinking. It said: "Sure – let me think about that for thirty seconds."* |
| Ending | From scarecrows to self-roasts – AI comedy is growing up. |

- Gorenz & Schwarz, PLOS ONE (Jul 2024): ~70% of participants rated ChatGPT-3.5's jokes funnier than human-written ones. https://today.usc.edu/ai-jokes-chatgpt-humor-study/
