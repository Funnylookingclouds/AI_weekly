# Script draft – AI Bounce SPECIAL EDITION 3: "How much electricity does AI really use?"

Status: DRAFT – awaiting approval. Debate format: Rune (data nerd) vs. Ture (sceptic), all four at the end.

| Part | Who | Text |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | Rune | Is AI eating the world's electricity? Let's check the real numbers. |
| 1 | Ture | It sure sounds like it! Data centers used about **485 terawatt-hours** in **2025**. |
| 2 | Rune | True – that's roughly **one and a half percent** of all the world's electricity. But **AI** is only part of it. AI-focused data centers used about **155 terawatt-hours**. That's around **half a percent**. |
| 3 | Ture | Half a percent? So it's **no problem** at all? |
| 4 | Rune | Not so fast. The **IEA** expects data centers to nearly **double by 2030** – to about **three percent** of the world's power. |
| 5 | Ture | So: **small today**, but **growing fast**. |
| Ending | All four | Half a percent now – watch this space. |

## Facts & sources
- Data centres ≈ 485 TWh in 2025 (up from ≈ 415 TWh in 2024) – IEA update, Apr 2026 (via Our World in Data / Carbon Brief).
- 485 TWh ≈ 1.5% of global electricity (IEA framing; global ≈ 31,000 TWh).
- AI-focused data centres ≈ 155 TWh in 2025 = 0.49% of global electricity – Our World in Data (IEA data).
- IEA projection: data centres ≈ 950 TWh by 2030 ≈ 3% of global demand (485 → 950 ≈ ×1.96, "nearly double").
- Independent: Nature paper projects AI data centres at ~1% of global electricity by 2030 (lower than IEA's AI path) – mentioned in description.
